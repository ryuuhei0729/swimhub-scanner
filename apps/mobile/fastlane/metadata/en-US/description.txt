SwimHub Scanner digitizes handwritten swim time sheets — just photograph them with your camera.

[AI-powered auto-reading]
AI analyses the photo of your time sheet and automatically extracts names, menus, strokes, and every time. You can also pick an image from your photo library.

[Menu recognition]
Distance, reps, sets, circle time and other practice menu details are read together.

[Edit the results]
Tap to easily fix swimmer names, strokes (Fr / Br / Ba / Fly / IM) and times. You can add or remove swimmers too.

[Automatic time stats]
Average, fastest and slowest times are calculated automatically. The fastest is highlighted in blue and the slowest in red, so you can grasp performance at a glance.

[Three export formats]
- Image (PNG) — share directly on social media or chat
- CSV — process freely in spreadsheet software
- Excel — outputs menu details and time records in a two-sheet layout

[Perfect for]
- Coaches who want to speed up time entry after practice
- Teams that keep handwritten records but also want digital management
- Parents and managers who want to organise meet and practice records in Excel

[Account and pricing]
Create an account easily with Apple, Google or email. Scanning is available every day even on the free plan.
